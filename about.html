ABOUT STEEL PLUSH RUN

Small plushies. Big adventures.

Our Story

Steel Plush Run was created in 2024 by a group of anime fans who wanted to bring their favorite characters to life in a new way.

Our idea was simple: turn legendary characters into soft, collectible plushies that fans could keep on their desks, shelves, or take on adventures.

Today, we offer a growing collection of plushies inspired by unforgettable anime characters. Every plush has its own personality and story.
